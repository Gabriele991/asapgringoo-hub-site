AsapGringoo Hub is a personal desktop application that runs only on its owner's computer. It helps the owner manage their own YouTube channel, @Asapgringoo: preparing clips of their own live streams, uploading them to their own channel after explicit approval of each post, checking the status of those uploads, and viewing the channel's own statistics.

The app is not offered to the public, has no accounts, no servers and no tracking of visitors or users. It has a single user: its owner.
