What AsapGringoo Hub is

AsapGringoo Hub is a desktop application that runs only on the owner's own computer. It has a single user, the owner of the YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and has no accounts, no servers and no tracking of visitors or users.

What the app accesses on YouTube

With the owner's explicit consent (Google OAuth 2.0, installed-app flow) the app requests three scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload - to upload videos to the owner's own channel. Every upload starts only after the owner has reviewed and explicitly authorized that post.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly - to read the processing and privacy status of the videos the app itself uploaded, and the name of the connected channel.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly - to read the statistics (for example views and watch time) of the owner's own channel and videos, shown to the owner inside the app.

What the app stores, and where

  • The OAuth tokens, in a file on the owner's computer, outside the application folder. They are used only to call the YouTube APIs.
  • The ID and status of videos uploaded by the app, the post text written by the owner, the statistics shown in the app, and a counter of API quota used per day, in a local database on the same computer.
  • The app does not store login credentials (username or password), does not collect data about viewers or other channels, and does not copy YouTube data to any server. All data stays on the owner's PC.

What the app shares

Nothing. No data is sent to third parties, sold, or used for advertising. The only network traffic is the direct communication with Google/YouTube servers needed to sign in, upload and read the owner's own data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

AsapGringoo Hub's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The app also uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your control: revoking access and deleting data

  • Revoke the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions; the app can then no longer reach the channel.
  • Delete the token file and the local database on the computer to remove everything the app holds. Data is deleted immediately on request of the owner; no copy exists elsewhere.

Children

The app uploads the owner's own videos and always declares them as not made for kids. It does not collect personal data from children.
